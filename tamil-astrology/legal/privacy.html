Privacy Policy

Astrosoft Tamil (ஆஸ்ட்ரோசாஃப்ட் தமிழ்) · Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Astrosoft Tamil mobile application (the "App"), published by AAATechnologies ("we", "us", "our"), handles your information. We designed the App to work on your device: the birth details and profiles you enter stay on your device, and we do not sell your information or use it for advertising. To understand how the App is used and to fix crashes, the App uses Google Firebase services, described in sections 4 and 5.

1. Information you enter

To generate horoscopes, matchmaking, and other results, you may enter details such as name, gender, date of birth, time of birth, and place of birth, for yourself or for others. This information:

  • is stored only on your device, in the App's private storage;
  • is never sent to us or to any server, and is not included in analytics or crash reports;
  • is excluded from cloud (Google Drive) backup — the App explicitly opts out, so your birth details are never uploaded to Google's servers via Android Auto Backup;
  • may be transferred to a new device via Android's local device-to-device transfer feature, which operates entirely over a local connection without going through any cloud server;
  • is deleted when you delete a profile in the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

2. Place search and location

When you search for a birth place or location, the App first looks it up in a built-in list of cities on your device. If there is no match, the App uses Android's built-in geocoding service to find the place's coordinates. That service is provided by your device platform (on most devices, Google), and the search text you type may be sent to it. Such processing is governed by the provider's privacy policy, for example the Google Privacy Policy.

The App does not track your location in the background, and we do not receive or store your location.

3. PDF reports

PDF reports you export are created on your device. If you choose to save or share a report, it is handled by the app or destination you pick, under that service's own terms.

4. Analytics and app configuration

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand which features are used, so we can improve the App. It collects:

  • screens you open and actions you take in the App, such as generating a horoscope, calculating a match, exporting a PDF, or changing a setting (including the language, date and time format, and the default city chosen in Settings);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate country derived from the network;
  • identifiers: the Firebase app-instance ID and, where your device allows it, the Android Advertising ID.

Analytics events do not include names, dates, times, or places of birth, or any other profile details you enter.

The App also uses Google Firebase Remote Config to turn features on or off without an app update. This sends a Firebase installation ID and basic app and device information to Google.

The App does not show advertising and we do not use this data to create a profile of you for advertising. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

5. Crash reporting

The App uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes and errors (for example, a failed PDF export) so we can fix them. A crash report contains the error details, the state of the App at the time, device model, operating system version, app version, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. Crash reports do not include your name or the profile details you enter.

6. Google as service provider

Firebase Analytics, Remote Config, and Crashlytics are provided by Google, which processes this data on our behalf. Data is sent over encrypted connections. Analytics data is kept for 2 months, and Crashlytics reports for up to 90 days. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Google Play

The App is purchased and installed through Google Play. Google may collect information related to your purchase and device under the Google Privacy Policy. We also receive the aggregated statistics and crash reports that Google Play Console provides to developers.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

Your profiles stay in the App's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. The App explicitly excludes them from Google Drive cloud backup. Please protect your device with a screen lock.

10. Your choices

You can view, edit, or delete saved profiles at any time in the App. Uninstalling the App removes all data it has stored on your device. To ask us to delete analytics or crash data linked to your device, contact us at the address below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
