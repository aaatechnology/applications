About

Astrosoft Tamil (ஆஸ்ட்ரோசாஃப்ட் தமிழ்) is a Vedic astrology app available in Tamil and English. All calculations run on your device.

Features
- Panchangam (daily almanac)
- Horoscope and divisional charts, dasa periods, planetary strength and yogas
- Matchmaking (porutham)
- Muhurtha (auspicious timings)
- Transit (gochar) analysis
- Numerology
- PDF export of horoscope and matchmaking reports
