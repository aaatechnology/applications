About this app

Astrology Tamil (ஜோதிட தமிழ்) is a complete Vedic astrology app for Tamil and English speakers. All calculations run entirely on your device – no internet connection required for horoscopes or charts.

★ Panchangam
Daily almanac with tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, and rahu/yama kalam for any date and location.

★ Horoscope
Rasi and divisional charts (D1–D12), dasa periods, planetary strength (shadbala), and yogas.

★ Matchmaking (Porutham)
Full 10-porutham compatibility analysis for marriage matching.

★ Muhurtha
Auspicious timing finder for ceremonies, travel, and important events.

★ More features
Transit (gochar) analysis, numerology, and PDF export of horoscope and matchmaking reports.
